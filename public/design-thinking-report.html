1.4 Problem Statement

📌

"Engineering students at Rajalakshmi Engineering College lack a centralized, trustworthy platform to access, organize, and discover quality study materials — resulting in exam anxiety, wasted time, and academic underperformance."

The problem is real and pressing. Students resort to WhatsApp groups, scattered Google Drive links, and pirated PDFs that are often unverified, outdated, or incomplete. There is no role-based system to organize uploads, and no quality gate to validate materials.

1.5 Case Study Learning Applied

We studied Google Classroom and Moodle, two widely adopted academic platforms:

PlatformStrengthLimitationWhat We Learned
Google ClassroomIntegrated with DriveNo notes discovery, no ratingsNeed dedicated search + quality indicators
MoodleFeature-rich LMSComplex UI, slow adoptionSimplicity and modern design drive engagement
💡

Learning Applied: Simplicity of access, role-based permissions, and a visually engaging interface are the key differentiators for student adoption. Notezilla was designed to feel as intuitive as Instagram but structured like a library.

1.6 Student Reflection

  • Empathy is the foundation — Initial assumptions about what students needed (more storage) were wrong. Real interviews revealed the actual need was discoverability and trust.
  • Iteration beats perfection — The first wireframe was discarded after user feedback. Willingness to restart made the final product significantly better.
  • Technology serves humans — The tech stack (Node.js, Supabase, JWT) was chosen based on user needs, not on what the team already knew.
  • Testing reveals invisible flaws — A bug in the faculty filter that went unnoticed was immediately caught during the first live user test session.

2.2 User Persona

👩‍💻
Priya Rajkumar
Age 20 · 2nd Year CSE · Rajalakshmi Engineering College

Goals

  • Find verified, unit-wise notes before exams
  • Know which faculty uploaded which materials
  • Save favourite notes for quick access later
  • Track what she has studied vs. what is pending

  • Can't find notes for a specific unit (e.g., Unit 3)
